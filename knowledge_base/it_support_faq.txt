# 🖥️ IT Support & Hardware Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Laptop & Display Setup
- **USB-C Docking Station:** Connect the single USB-C cable to your laptop. If external display displays "No Signal", unplug dock power for 10 seconds, then reconnect.
- **Mac Display Mirroring:** Go to System Settings > Displays > Arrange to extend or mirror desktops.

## 2. Company VPN Setup
- **GlobalProtect VPN:** Download GlobalProtect from `https://vpn.company.internal`.
- **Portal Server:** Enter `vpn.company.com`.
- **Authentication:** Use your SSO email and 2FA Authenticator app code.

## 3. Password Reset Policy
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- Reset your password at `https://id.company.internal/reset`.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include uppercase, numbers, and symbols.

# 🏢 Company Policies & Employee Handbook

## 1. Work From Home & Remote Allowance
- Employees receive a **$500 one-time stipend** for home office equipment (monitors, ergonomic chairs).
- Submit receipts via the **Billing / General** ticket category in the Support Desk.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Sick Leave
- Standard PTO accrual: **20 days per year**.
- Request PTO via HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 3. Expense Reimbursements
- Meals during business travel are capped at **$75/day**.
- All expenses over $25 require an itemized receipt.
